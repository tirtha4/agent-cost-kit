How the estimate works

The run is priced call by call. Call N sends the system prompt, the tool definitions, the task and the text and results of calls 1 to N−1. A cached call pays 10% of the input price for the prefix it shares with the previous call, and 125% to write the rest. Thinking and visible output are billed as output. Parallel calls group independent reads (3 per call), stopping early skips 3 re-checks, filtering keeps 6 tools, trimming sends 1,000-token results, pruning keeps the last 3 results and summarises older ones in 150 tokens, and low effort thinks 400 tokens instead of 1,500. It's a model, not your invoice: use the kit's benchmark to measure your own agent.
